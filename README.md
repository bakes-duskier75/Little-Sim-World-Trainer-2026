## 🎮 About the Game

Little Sim World is a cozy 2D life simulation game developed and published by.GRAVITY. It launched in Early Access on May 1, 2025, and reached version 1.0 on August 17, 2026. Set in Little London, it combines open-world exploration, character customization, home decoration, relationships, careers, skills, university life, and everyday needs management.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Little Sim World**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life enhancements for Little Sim World, including unlimited crowns, frozen needs, faster skill progression, relationship assistance, and inventory management. It is designed for offline single-player use and complements the game's life-simulation mechanics without adding combat features.

**Target Audience:** For players who want to experiment with character builds, focus on decorating and storytelling, reduce routine management, accelerate skill and relationship progression, or explore Little London at their own pace.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Crowns** — Prevents crowns from decreasing when shopping, eating out, completing home projects, or purchasing furniture and clothing.
- **Freeze Needs** — Locks the Little's health, happiness, comfort, hunger, thirst, energy, hygiene, and bladder values at their current levels.
- **Restore Needs** — Refills the Little's managed needs to help maintain a balanced daily routine.
- **Skill Experience Multiplier** — Increases experience gained from activities such as cooking, fishing, painting, writing, and exercising.
- **Instant Skill Levels** — Allows selected interests and skills to reach their next level immediately.
- **Perk Progress Assistance** — Speeds progression toward skill-based rewards, including perks, new interactions, and unlockable outfits.
- **Relationship Progress** — Improves relationship progression with residents and helps unlock higher relationship levels and social interactions.
- **Inventory Quantity** — Prevents selected usable items, ingredients, collectibles, and resources from being consumed.
- **Movement Speed** — Adjusts the Little's movement speed for faster travel around the seamless Little London map.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **No Combat Mode** — Little Sim World does not feature a conventional combat system, so combat-related trainer functions are not applicable.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Start the game and load into Little London before activating movement options. Use the movement-speed feature for quicker trips between shops, parks, the lake, university, and other locations. Avoid changing speed during transitions or while interacting with doors, menus, or activities.

### Tools & Inventory

Activate inventory protection after the Little has loaded and the inventory screen is available. Use it for ingredients, food, collectibles, and other selected items. Confirm that the desired item is supported before relying on the option for quests or crafting, and disable it before saving if an item count behaves unexpectedly.

### World & Environment

Little Sim World centers on a single customizable character living in an open neighborhood. Explore shops, cafés, the pub, museum, library, park, university, lake, and notice boards. The trainer can reduce travel and upkeep demands while leaving exploration, requests, home customization, careers, and social choices intact.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no standard combat gameplay in Little Sim World. The game focuses on life simulation, relationships, skills, careers, customization, collecting, cooking, fishing, painting, writing, and home improvement. Combat hotkeys and damage options are therefore unavailable.
